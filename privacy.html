1

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly and information generated when you use HopOn:

  • Account Info: Name, email address, phone number, profile photo
  • Student Verification: University email address (.edu) for identity verification
  • Location Data: Pickup and drop-off locations when booking rides or posting cargo shipments
  • Cargo Details: Package descriptions, dimensions, weight, declared value
  • Communications: Messages sent through the in-app chat feature
  • Infos de compte : Nom, adresse e-mail, numéro de téléphone, photo de profil
  • Vérification étudiante : E-mail universitaire (.edu) pour l'accès aux réductions
  • Données de localisation : Lieux de prise en charge et de dépose pour les trajets/colis
  • Détails du colis : Description du paquet, dimensions, poids, valeur déclarée
  • Communications : Messages échangés via la messagerie de l'application
2

How We Use Your Information

Your data helps us deliver a safe, reliable ridesharing and cargo delivery experience:

  • Matching passengers and cargo requests with available drivers on their routes
  • Processing bookings, cargo shipments, and payments securely
  • Sending ride and delivery confirmations, status updates, and safety alerts
  • Improving app performance, safety, and user experience
  • Mettre en correspondance les passagers et les expéditeurs avec les conducteurs disponibles
  • Traiter de manière sécurisée les réservations, les envois de colis et les paiements
  • Envoyer des confirmations de trajet, des mises à jour de statut et des alertes de sécurité
  • Améliorer les performances de l'application, sa sécurité et l'expérience utilisateur
We never sell your personal data
3

Data Sharing

We only share your data with other users (first name and vehicle info), payment processors (Stripe), map services (Google Maps), and safety or legal authorities when required.

4

Location Data

HopOn only accesses your location when you actively use the app to post or book a ride. We do not track your location in the background. You can disable location access at any time in your device settings.

5

Data Security

We implement TLS/SSL encryption for data transmission and hash user passwords using bcrypt. Regular security reviews are performed to protect personal information.

6

Data Retention

Profile data is deleted within 30 days of account cancellation. Historical logs (trips, cargo matched routes) can be kept up to 12 months for safety and tax validation. Financial logs are kept for 7 years.

7

Your Rights

You have the right to request access, correction, or deletion of all personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at hoponboardhelp@gmail.com.

8

Cookies, Tracking & CCPA Disclosure

HopOn uses cookies to manage sessions. Under California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you can opt-out of analytical cookies at any time through your browser's DNT headers or settings page.

9

Children's Privacy

HopOn is intended for users aged 17 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 17. If detected, accounts are deleted immediately.

10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will send an email or in-app notification to users 14 days before material changes take effect.

11

Email & CAN-SPAM Compliance

All automated and promotional emails we send include an unsubscribe link, non-misleading subjects, and physical location information. Opt-out requests are processed within 10 business days.

Questions about your privacy?

Our privacy team responds within 30 days. We take every query seriously.

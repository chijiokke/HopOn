Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 26, 2026  ·  Last Updated: June 26, 2026

At HopOn, your privacy matters deeply. This policy explains exactly what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how we use it, and the rights you have over your information. We are committed to transparency and protecting your data.

📋 1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly and information generated when you use HopOn:

  • Account Info: Name, email address, phone number, profile photo
  • Student Verification: University email address (.edu) for optional identity verification and discount access
  • Location Data: Pickup and drop-off locations when booking rides or posting cargo shipments
  • Ride & Cargo History: Past trips, cargo shipments, routes, timestamps, and matched partners
  • Cargo Details: Package descriptions, dimensions, weight, declared value, and recipient names/contact numbers
  • Delivery Verification Photos: Image/photo proofs uploaded by senders and drivers at cargo pickup and drop-off
  • Payment Info: Processed securely via third-party providers (we never store card numbers)
  • Communications: Messages sent through the in-app chat feature
  • Device Info: Device type, operating system, browser type, and IP address
  • Usage Data: Features you use, buttons clicked, time spent in app

🎯 2. How We Use Your Information

Your data helps us deliver a safe, reliable ridesharing and cargo delivery experience:

  • Matching passengers and cargo requests with available drivers on their routes
  • Processing bookings, cargo shipments, and payments securely
  • Verifying student identity to unlock student discounts and badges
  • Sending ride and delivery confirmations, status updates, and safety alerts
  • Enabling in-app messaging between drivers, passengers, and senders
  • Verifying cargo delivery state and receipt via user-submitted verification photos
  • Improving app performance, safety, and user experience
  • Detecting and preventing fraudulent, illegal, or unsafe activity
  • Complying with legal obligations
We never sell your personal data to third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

🤝 3. Data Sharing

We only share your data in limited circumstances:

  • Other Users: Your first name, rating, and vehicle info are shared with your matched ride partner
  • Payment Processors: Stripe or similar providers to handle transactions securely
  • Map Services: Google Maps for route visualization (subject to Google's privacy policy)
  • Safety Authorities: Law enforcement when legally required or to protect user safety
  • Service Providers: Hosting, analytics, and support tools under strict data agreements

📍 4. Location Data

HopOn only accesses your location when you actively use the app to post or book a ride. We do not track your location in the background.

Location data is used solely to match rides, display routes, and improve pickup accuracy. You can disable location access at any time in your device settings, though some features may be limited.

🔒 5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • All data transmitted using TLS/SSL encryption
  • Passwords hashed using bcrypt — never stored in plain text
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability testing
  • Access to personal data limited to authorised personnel only
  • Two-factor authentication available for all accounts

While we take every precaution, no system is 100% secure. Please use a strong, unique password and report any suspicious activity immediately.

⏳ 6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account:

  • Profile data is deleted within 30 days
  • Ride and cargo shipment history (including route details and delivery verification photos) may be retained for up to 12 months for safety, dispute resolution, and audit validation
  • Financial records are kept for 7 years as required by law
  • Chat messages are deleted within 90 days

⚖️ 7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Update inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection: Opt out of certain types of data processing
  • Restriction: Request we limit how we use your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hoponboardhelp@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

🍪 8. Cookies, Tracking & CCPA Disclosure

HopOn uses cookies and local storage to keep you logged in, remember preferences, and analyze app performance. We also utilize third-party analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to help us understand app usage.

CCPA / California Privacy Note: Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), the use of third-party analytics cookies may be classified as "selling" or "sharing" of personal information. You can opt-out of these cookies at any time through our cookie settings page or your browser's Do Not Track (DNT) header.

You can clear or block cookies at any time through your browser settings. Note that disabling essential functional cookies may limit certain app features.

👶 9. Children's Privacy

HopOn is intended for users aged 17 and older (including students and general commuters). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 17.

If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us immediately at hoponboardhelp@gmail.com and we will delete the account promptly.

🔄 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will:

  • Send an in-app notification and email to all users
  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page
  • Require re-acceptance for material changes affecting your rights

Continued use of HopOn after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

✉️ 11. Email & CAN-SPAM Compliance

We comply fully with CAN-SPAM regulations for all emails. Every automated notification or promotional email we send includes: (1) A clear, one-click unsubscribe link; (2) Non-misleading subject lines; and (3) Our physical mailing address listed below. We process opt-out requests within 10 business days.

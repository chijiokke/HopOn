📦 12. Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Cargo Delivery Guidelines & Policies

HopOn provides a peer-to-peer parcel and cargo delivery marketplace connecting users (senders) who want to ship items with drivers traveling to the same destination. This service is designed strictly for cost-sharing and community-driven convenience. By posting or accepting a cargo shipment request, you agree to the following policies:

  • Eligibility & Purpose: Anyone who meets HopOn's general eligibility requirements can send or transport cargo. Cargo transport must be on a cost-sharing basis. Drivers are not commercial couriers and must not charge commercial courier rates.
  • Package Dimensions & Placement: Cargo must easily fit in the vehicle’s trunk or backseat. Senders must provide accurate package size and weight details. Parcels must not obstruct the driver’s rear visibility, interfere with passenger seating, or compromise vehicle safety.
  • Secure Packaging & Labeling: The sender is solely responsible for packing the cargo securely in a sealed box, mailer, or container. The parcel must be clearly labeled with the recipient’s name, delivery address, and telephone number. Drivers are not liable for damage resulting from improper or fragile packaging.
  • Right to Inspect: To ensure safety and legality, **drivers have the absolute right to open and inspect the contents of any cargo package** before accepting it for transport. Senders must cooperate with this inspection. If a sender refuses inspection, the driver must decline the transport.
  • Prohibited Items: Senders are strictly prohibited from shipping, and drivers must refuse to transport, the following items:
    • Hazardous materials, flammables, explosives, fireworks, or toxic/corrosive chemicals.
    • Illegal substances, narcotics, prescription drugs, alcohol, or tobacco products.
    • Weapons, ammunition, or military-grade equipment.
    • Live animals, biological material, or human remains.
    • Perishable food items prone to rapid spoilage, melting, or emitting offensive odors.
    • Items exceeding a combined market value of $1,000 CAD (including cash, jewelry, and high-end electronics).
  • Declared Value & Liability Cap: Senders must enter an accurate declared value before submitting a cargo request. **The maximum declared value is strictly capped at $1,000 CAD.** HopOn operates solely as a matching marketplace and is not a common carrier. Any damage, loss, or theft during carriage is the responsibility of the driver and sender, and the maximum liability of the driver or HopOn for any cargo-related dispute is capped at the declared value, up to a maximum of $1,000 CAD. Senders ship high-value items at their own risk.
  • Pickup & Drop-off Protocol: Senders must be present at pickup to hand over the parcel, and the recipient must be present at drop-off to receive the parcel. If the recipient does not show up within 10 minutes of the driver’s arrival, the driver may arrange to return the package to the sender (at the sender's cost) or leave it in a safe location after consulting the sender. Additional fees may apply for returned items.
  • Photo Verification: Senders and drivers must upload a photo confirmation of the parcel at pickup and drop-off to document the condition of the package and verify successful delivery.

1. Common Definitions

Throughout these Terms, the following definitions apply:

  • Member: A Driver, a Passenger, a Cargo Sender, or any other registered user of the Service.
  • Driver: A Member offering empty seats and/or cargo space in their vehicle to share with other Members during a Trip.
  • Passenger: A Member booking seats in a Driver's vehicle.
  • Account: A personalized profile created by a Member to access and use the Service.
  • Trip: A route planned from an origin to a destination at a specific date and time, created and maintained by a Driver.
  • Booking: The act of paying the Contribution and associated Booking Fee through the Service prior to departure.
  • Request: A post by a Passenger looking for a ride, or a Sender looking to transport cargo between locations on a specific date.
  • Contribution: The sum of money agreed and paid by a Passenger or Cargo Sender to a Driver to cover vehicle running costs. Drivers must not make a profit.
  • Booking Fee: A platform service fee added on top of the Contribution and paid by the Passenger/Sender when booking.
  • Payout: The cleared Contributions sent to a Driver's linked Payout Method.
  • Payout Method: The linked financial account (Direct Deposit or PayPal) used to receive Payouts.
  • Cancellation Allowance: The privilege for Drivers to cancel a maximum of one Trip with approved Bookings (or one approved Booking) every six months without penalty.
  • Carpool / Carpooling: Sharing a Trip in a private vehicle between a Driver and Passenger(s) in exchange for a Contribution.
  • Membre : Un conducteur, un passager, un expéditeur de colis ou tout autre utilisateur enregistré du Service.
  • Conducteur : Un membre offrant des places libres ou de l'espace de chargement dans son véhicule pour partager avec d'autres membres lors d'un trajet.
  • Passager : Un membre réservant des places dans le véhicule d'un conducteur.
  • Compte : Un profil personnalisé créé par un membre pour accéder et utiliser le Service.
  • Trajet : Un itinéraire planifié d'un point d'origine à une destination à une date et heure précises, créé par un conducteur.
  • Réservation : L'acte de payer la contribution et les frais de réservation associés via le Service avant le départ.
  • Demande : Une publication d'un passager cherchant un trajet, ou d'un expéditeur cherchant à transporter un colis.
  • Contribution : La somme d'argent convenue et payée par un passager ou un expéditeur à un conducteur pour couvrir les frais de fonctionnement du véhicule. Les conducteurs ne doivent pas faire de bénéfices.
  • Frais de réservation : Des frais de service de la plateforme ajoutés à la contribution et payés par le passager/expéditeur lors de la réservation.
  • Versement : Les contributions débloquées et envoyées à la méthode de versement liée du conducteur.
  • Méthode de versement : Le compte financier associé (dépôt direct ou PayPal) utilisé pour recevoir les versements.
  • Marge d'annulation : Le privilège pour les conducteurs d'annuler au maximum un trajet avec réservations approuvées tous les six mois sans pénalité.
  • Covoiturage : Le fait de partager un trajet dans un véhicule privé entre un conducteur et des passagers en échange d'une contribution.

3. Communication & Messaging Rules

HopOn provides an in-app messaging tool to facilitate safe coordinates between Members. You are strictly prohibited from exchanging personal contact information (such as phone numbers, email addresses, or external links) prior to a Booking being confirmed. Off-platform contacts before booking can result in account suspension.

4. Rides & Booking Systems

Drivers can manage bookings via **Request to Book** (manual approval in 1-24h window) or **Instant Book** (immediate capture). Drivers must be the primary driver, cannot delegate driving without disclosure, and must ensure enough seats and seatbelts are available.

5. Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Cargo Delivery Guidelines & Policies

HopOn cargo platform allows package delivery matching. Packages must fit easily, be properly labeled, and are subject to inspection. Senders are prohibited from shipping weapons, hazardous chemicals, narcotics, live animals, or items exceeding $1,000 CAD. Drivers hold the absolute right to inspect all cargo before accepting.

6. Payments & Fees

All payments are handled online via Stripe. Cash or off-platform payments (like e-transfers) are strictly prohibited and will lead to account closure. HopOn charges booking fees added on top of driver contributions. Held funds do not accrue interest. Users must cooperate with fraud checks.

7. Driver Payouts & Conditions

Payouts are sent to Canadian bank accounts ($CAD) or PayPal. Payouts are subject to a **4-day clearing escrow period** to resolve potential passenger disputes. Funds must be claimed within 365 days or are forfeited. Drivers must arrive 5 minutes early, disclose trip changes, and operate safely.

8. Cancellation & No-Show Policies

Passenger cancellations >24 hours receive a full fare refund (HopOn keeps booking fee). Cancellations <24 hours grant the driver 50% fare. Passenger no-shows grant 100% fare. Drivers are limited to one cancellation every six months (Cancellation Allowance); exceeding it leads to review. Disputes must be filed within 4 days.

9. Driver's License & Insurance Requirements

Drivers must hold a full, unexpired license (probationary or restricted licenses are not accepted). Vehicles must be fully insured and meet road safety standards. Drivers and passengers are responsible for verifying ridesharing insurance coverage.

10. Driver Priority Responsibility

The Driver holds the ultimate authority to accept bookings. If a Driver accepts out-of-terms arrangements (like additional cash demands or alternative pick-ups), HopOn will refund passengers and reverse payouts—even if the passenger requested the arrangement.

11. Safety & Conduct

All members must wear seatbelts, not use substances, and behave respectfully. Drivers must use hands-free devices only. Report unsafe behavior immediately to authorities and support.

12. Community Agreement & Policies

We operate a zero-tolerance community for discrimination (based on race, age, sex, orientation) or harassment of any form. Sexual harassment will result in permanent account closure. Reports are handled confidentially.

13. Identity Verification

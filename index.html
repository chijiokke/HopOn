The Story Behind HopOn
It started with a moment I'll never forget. I was a broke college student watching all my mates pack up their bags and head home for the holidays. The residence halls were emptying out, everyone excited to see their families.

I had been saving up, but I still couldn't afford the commute to my sister's place across town. While everyone else was going home, I had to stay back at the residence — alone, over the holidays. Just watching my mates leave, one by one.

That moment hit deep. I thought: "There has to be a better way. What if we could share rides? What if getting home didn't have to cost a fortune?"

HopOn was born from that frustration — and the belief that no one should ever be left behind because they can't afford a ride.

Today, HopOn is open to everyone — students, workers, commuters, families, and anyone who believes getting around should be affordable, safe, and community-driven.

Frequently Asked Questions
How do I book a ride? ▼
Go to the Rides tab, browse available rides, tap a card to expand it, then tap "Hop On". Your booking is confirmed instantly and the driver is notified.
How do I post a ride? ▼
Tap the Rides tab → "Give a Ride" tab → fill in your route, date, time, and seats → tap "Post Ride". Your listing goes live immediately.
How does payment work? ▼
Payment is handled securely through the app. The agreed amount is charged when you book and released to the driver after the ride is completed. No cash needed.
Can I cancel a booking? ▼
Yes — go to Trips → tap your active trip → Cancel. Free cancellations must be made at least 1 hour before pickup. Late cancellations may incur a small fee.
How do I get my 15% student discount? ▼
Profile → Student Verification → enter your .edu email → Verify. Once confirmed you get 15% off all rides plus a verified badge on your profile.
What if I feel unsafe during a ride? ▼
Tap Profile → Safety Center → SOS Emergency button to alert authorities immediately. You can also share your live trip location with a trusted contact.
How do I get a refund? ▼
Refunds for cancelled rides arrive within 5–7 business days. For disputes, email hoponboardhelp@gmail.com within 48 hours of your ride.

Our support team responds within 2 hours.
